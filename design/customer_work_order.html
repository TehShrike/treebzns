Design notes

  • The work list is the same layout as the proposal. Photos and details do not change. The only difference is that declined items drop out and the price is settled.
  • The date block is the first thing on the page, because that is the one thing a customer comes back to check.
  • The date is a range, not a promise. When a project_crew row exists, it can say the day. Until then it is a guess from where the job sits in the queue.
  • The declined item is named but not priced. It gives the customer a way back in without making the page look like an upsell.
  • Open: how do we compute "the week of August 24" before a visit is scheduled? It needs the queue order and rough crew capacity. The design doc says to settle this while designing the schedule screen.
  • Open: "Before we come" is company text. Nothing in the schema holds it. Add a company setting, or leave it out of the first version?
  • Open: does the customer see progress after the first visit, when some line items are done (project_line_item.done_at) and others are not?
