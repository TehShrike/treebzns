# 2026-08-17 notes

## Discount descriptions

Invoice-level discounts and line-item-level discounts should each have a "discount description" varchar.

## Invoice-level percentage discount

The invoice-level discount needs a percentage option. A percentage discount and a flat amount discount are mutually exclusive — an invoice has one or the other, not both.

## Crew scheduling map

When scheduling crews, the map is mostly relevant for only one crew at a time. Zoom in on the map and stage projects, but only in the context of adding stuff to one crew.

## Project view line item navigation

In the project view, a table of contents on the left for selecting line items. Selecting one zooms you to the photo-centric view of that line item.

## Quote counts next to customers

Show "declined quotes" and "accepted quotes" next to every customer, for estimators and lead entry folks.

## Customer tagging system

A tagging system for customers, e.g. "good client", "bad lead".
