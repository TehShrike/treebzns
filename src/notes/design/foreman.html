Design notes

  • The list shows the crew's day in schedule order. Pinned jobs show a time, ordered jobs show "2nd stop". It matches what the office set on the schedule screen.
  • Directions, Call, and Text are the three actions the design doc asks for. They use the contact fields baked onto the project, so a later change to the client record does not move today's job.
  • The crew clock is one button. Under it, each person has a row, so a foreman can add somebody who showed up late without clocking the whole crew again.
  • Only a person with the clock permission sees the crew clock panel. Everybody else sees their own clock row. Crews have no modeled leader, so the permission is the gate.
  • Open: time_entry points at a project, not at a project_crew row. "Clock the crew off this job" needs to know which visit. src/notes/todo.md already lists this change.
  • Open: checking a line item off writes done_at. If a job takes two visits and only part of a line item is finished, is a checkbox enough?
  • Open: does the crew need to see the price? It is on the project, but showing money in the field may invite arguments at the door. This mockup hides it.
  • Open: this screen must work with no signal. Photos and clock actions need a queue. See "Resilliency to Cloudflare downtime" in src/notes/big_todos.md.
