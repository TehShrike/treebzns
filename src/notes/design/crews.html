    Design notes

    • Drag a person from "Everybody" onto a crew card to make them a regular. That writes a crew_regular row and a crew_regular_history row.
    • A person can be a regular on more than one crew. The schema allows it (crew_regular keys on crew and employee).
    • The dashed person on Crew 2 is borrowed for one day. It comes from project_crew_employee for that day's jobs. The card shows it so the roster page tells the truth about who is out with that crew.
    • The colored bar at the top of each card is crew.color. The same color marks the crew on the schedule board and the map.
    • The skill matrix is the fastest way to fix a gap: the schedule screen warns that no crew has Crane, and this table is where somebody gets that skill.
    • Open: crews have no leader in the schema. A permission decides who can clock a crew in and out. Should this screen show which crew members hold that permission?
    • Open: nothing marks a crew as inactive. When a crew stops running, do we delete it, or add crew.archived? Deleting breaks old project_crew rows.
