    Design notes

    • The design doc asks for four things: users, permissions, work skills, and line item templates. The Company tab holds the other codebooks that already exist in the schema, so they have a home.
    • Permissions are set per role, not per person. A person gets roles. This matches software_role_permission and keeps the person row small.
    • The user table shows crew and work skills, because that is what the office needs when somebody new starts. The crews screen is where the roster gets shuffled.
    • Work skills carry the hourly rate that the estimator's blended rate uses. Equipment is not a separate list.
    • Open: the permission list has no "clock a crew in and out" code. The foreman screen needs it. Add CAN_CLOCK_CREW?
    • Open: deleting a work skill or a template. Old projects point at them. Hide them instead of deleting, or refuse to delete when they are used?
    • Open: employee.email and login_name are unique across every company, not per company. Two companies can not both have a "dale". Is that the plan?
