Design notes

  • The page opens on the plain invoice, because a person who follows an invoice link wants to check the math and pay. The switch at the top brings back the photos and the full descriptions from the proposal.
  • The invoice rows are invoice_line_item rows, not project line items. They are copied at close time, so a later change to the project does not rewrite an invoice a customer already has.
  • The bill-to block reads from the invoice, for the same reason. The address is copied onto invoice.billing_* when the invoice is made.
  • The past tense in the detail view comes from the same project_line_item.work_details text. Nothing rewrites it. Only the heading changes: "What we will do" becomes "What we did".
  • Open: paying online is not built. src/notes/big_todos.md points at Stripe Connect. Until then the button should say how to pay instead.
  • Open: an invoice can cover part of a work order (invoice.project_id is nullable and line items map one at a time). Does the customer page show one invoice, or all invoices for the project?
  • Open: client credit (client_credit, invoice.client_credit_applied) does not show here. Should a credit appear as its own line in the totals?
